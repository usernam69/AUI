What's new

A study space, and a better desktop app October 21, 2026

  • Study space: flashcards that come back on a schedule, a mistake notebook, an exam planner that makes a calendar file, a study streak that forgives a missed day, badges and a weekly recap. It all stays in your browser.
  • Twenty-six built-in skills, including Quiz me, Flashcard maker, Essay feedback and coaches for math, history, chemistry, physics, biology, literature, Spanish and statistics.
  • Citations in APA, MLA and Chicago, with the in-text version.
  • Choose how you like things explained, and tell AUI about each class. A folder is a class.
  • Share a skill, a deck, a study plan or a chat as a link. The content travels inside the link; AUI keeps no copy.
  • Pick any OpenRouter model with its price, retry or compare an answer on another model, and see your credit. If a model is busy, AUI tries free ones instead, and errors come with a button that fixes them.
  • With a key, a photo of your homework goes to the model as a picture.
  • Student plan (demo checkout).
  • Desktop app: Ctrl+Shift+Space opens or hides it, Ctrl+Shift+A drops the text you copied into the message box, screenshots of part of your screen, a tray icon, start with the computer, and updates that install themselves.

Home page, skills and plans October 20, 2026

  • A home page at the start of the site, with a desktop app you can download.
  • Skills: thirteen built in, including Homework Helper, plus your own. Switch them on per chat or for every chat, and import or save them as SKILL.md files.
  • Plans with a demo checkout. It checks the card number with the Luhn test and takes no money.
  • Continue with Google on the sign-in screen.
  • Add your own OpenRouter key to answer with bigger models. Without one, AUI uses the small model on your device.
  • Privacy and security pages, passphrase encryption for saved chats, and a stricter Content Security Policy.

A big redesign October 18, 2026

  • A new ChatGPT-style look in blue, with an Appearance tab: accent colors, Ocean, Midnight, OLED, Sky and Sepia themes, fonts, bubble styles.
  • Pick your model: Tiny, Fastest, Balanced, Smart or Smartest. The model now runs in a background worker so the page stays smooth.
  • Faster replies: shorter prompts, reuse of work between turns, and smarter limits.
  • Sidebar: date groups, folders, tags, colors, emoji, drag to reorder pins, fuzzy search, bulk actions, trash, starred messages and a collapsible icon rail.
  • Composer: slash commands, per-chat drafts, smart paste, prompt improver, emoji, follow-up chips, message queue and push to talk.
  • Replies: run code, line numbers, Mermaid diagrams, charts, callouts, footnotes, task lists, sortable tables, TL;DR and version diffs.
  • Accurate math and unit conversions, plus optional Wikipedia, dictionary and weather lookups.
  • Fixed: updating AUI no longer deletes the downloaded model.

Model reliability October 2026

  • The model rebuilds itself after a failed generation, and copying handles clipboard errors.
  • Seventeen bug fixes from a deep review.
