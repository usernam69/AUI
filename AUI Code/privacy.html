Privacy

Short version: the AI runs on your device, your chats stay in your browser, and there are no ads, trackers or analytics.

Where your chats go

  • Nowhere, by default. AUI's model runs inside your browser. What you type, the files you attach and the replies never leave your device.
  • Your account, if you sign in. Signed-in chats, memories and settings sync to your own row in AUI's database (Supabase), which only your account can read. You can switch chat or memory sync off in your profile.
  • Homework Helper, only if you add your own key. This skill answers through OpenRouter. While it is on for a chat, what you type, the text of attached files and that chat's earlier messages go to OpenRouter and the model it picks, under your own OpenRouter account. They don't go to AUI. Your key stays in this browser and is never synced or exported.
  • Online lookups, only if you turn them on. The weather, dictionary and Wikipedia lookups send just the place, word or topic to those services.

What is stored on your device

Chats, memories, settings, knowledge files and the downloaded model, all in your browser's own storage. Settings, Privacy can encrypt them with a passphrase, delete old chats automatically, or clear chats when you close the browser. Settings, Model deletes the downloaded model.

No tracking

AUI has no analytics, ads or third-party trackers. The visitor counter on the sidebar sends a random ID that changes every day, so no lasting ID about you is stored. It's off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and you can turn it off in Settings, Privacy.
