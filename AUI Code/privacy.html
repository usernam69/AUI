Privacy

Short version: AUI runs a small model on your device by default, answers through your own OpenRouter key only if you add one, saves your chats in your browser, and has no ads, trackers or analytics.

Where your chats go

  • Nowhere, by default. AUI's small model runs inside your browser. What you type, the files you attach and the replies never leave your device.
  • To OpenRouter, only if you add your own key. Then each message is sent, with the earlier messages of that chat and the text of any files you attach, to OpenRouter and the model it picks, under your own OpenRouter account. It does not go to AUI. AUI never sees your key: it is saved in this browser only and is not synced, exported or encrypted by the passphrase vault. You can switch back in Settings, Model, AI engine.
  • Your account, if you sign in. Signed-in chats, memories and settings sync to your own row in AUI's database (Supabase), which only your account can read. You can switch chat or memory sync off in your profile.
  • Homework Helper always uses your key. Even with the model on this device chosen, this skill answers through OpenRouter.
  • Online lookups, only if you turn them on. The weather, dictionary and Wikipedia lookups send just the place, word or topic to those services.

What is stored on your device

Chats, memories, settings, knowledge files and the downloaded model, all in your browser's own storage. Settings, Privacy can encrypt them with a passphrase, delete old chats automatically, or clear chats when you close the browser. Settings, Model deletes the downloaded model.

No tracking

AUI has no analytics, ads or third-party trackers. The visitor counter on the sidebar sends a random ID that changes every day, so no lasting ID about you is stored. It's off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and you can turn it off in Settings, Privacy.
