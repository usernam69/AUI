AUI help

This page also works offline.

Getting started

AUI runs a small AI model inside your browser using WebGPU. The first reply downloads the model once (a few hundred MB to a few GB, depending on the speed you pick). After that it loads from your browser and works without internet. You don't need a key or an account. To use bigger models, add your own OpenRouter key in Settings → Model.

Use a recent desktop Chrome, Edge or Safari. On phones, Chrome on Android works best.

Change it in Settings → Model → Model speed, or type /fast, /balanced or /smart.

Commands

Type / in the message box to see them all, including /new, /temp, /explore, /improve, /save, /stats, /timer 25 and /export.

Skills

A skill is a short set of instructions AUI follows in its answers. Open Skills from the ✧ button next to the message box, or type / and a skill's name. Turn a skill on for one chat or for every chat. Twenty-six are built in, and you can write your own, share one as a link, or import a SKILL.md file. Homework Helper always answers through your OpenRouter key.

Study space

Open Study in the sidebar, or type /study. Cards are flashcards: press Make cards under any reply, paste notes, or add one by hand. Each card comes back after 1, 3 and 7 days if you say you knew it. Mistakes keeps replies you saved with Save mistake, so you can try them again. Plan (/exams) turns exam dates and topics into a day-by-day plan you can download as a calendar file. Classes lets you tell AUI about a class once; every chat in a folder with that name starts with it. Progress shows your streak, badges and a weekly summary you can copy for a parent or teacher. /cite makes APA, MLA and Chicago citations. All of it stays in this browser.

Share links

A skill, a flashcard deck, a study plan or a chat can be shared as a link. The content is inside the link, after the #, which browsers never send to a server. Whoever opens it reads it in their own browser and chooses whether to add it. Very long chats are too big for a link.

Models and your key

With an OpenRouter key you can pick any model (/model), retry or compare a reply on another model, and see your credit (/credits). If the chosen model is busy or out of credit, AUI tries the biggest free models. Pictures are sent to the model as pictures when a key answers.

Desktop app

Ctrl+Shift+Space opens or hides AUI from anywhere. Ctrl+Shift+A puts the text you copied into the message box. Screenshot in the attachment menu lets you drag over part of your screen. Closing the window keeps AUI in the tray; turn that off in Settings, General.

Privacy

Your messages are answered by a small model on your device. If you add your own OpenRouter key (Settings, Model), they are answered through OpenRouter instead. Chats are saved in this browser, and in your account only if you log in. Optional "Online lookups" (off by default) send weather, definition and "who/what is" questions to Open-Meteo, a free dictionary and Wikipedia.

Troubleshooting

  • "Needs WebGPU": update your browser, or try Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  • Download stuck: keep the tab open; parts already downloaded are kept if you stop.
  • Slow replies: pick a smaller model, or run the speed test from /stats.
  • Out of space: Settings → Data controls → Delete models.
