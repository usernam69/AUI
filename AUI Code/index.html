An AI that lives on your device.

AUI runs a language model inside your browser. Nothing you type is sent to an AI company, and using it costs nothing.

Start chatting Continue with Google

No account needed. The first reply downloads the model once; after that it works offline.

Your chats stay where you typed them.

It runs here. The model downloads to your browser once, then answers on your own processor and graphics chip. There is no AUI server reading your messages.

It remembers privately. Chats and memory are saved in this browser. Add a passphrase and they are encrypted before they are stored.

It tracks nothing. No ads, no analytics profile. An account is optional, and only needed if you want your chats on more than one device.

Teach it how you work.

A skill is a short set of instructions that AUI follows in every answer. Turn one on for a chat, or keep it always on.

Twelve come built in: summarizing, proofreading, translating, reviewing code, tutoring, meeting notes and more. Write your own for the jobs you repeat, or import a SKILL.md file.

Three plans. None of them charges you.

AUI is free. Plans change how many custom skills you can keep and add a badge to your profile. The checkout is a demo: it checks that a card number is well formed with the Luhn test, takes no money, and keeps only the card brand and last four digits.
