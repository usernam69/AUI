Your chats stay with you.

It runs here by default. The model downloads to your browser once, then answers on your own processor and graphics chip. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Your key, if you want more. Add an OpenRouter key and messages go from your browser to the model you pick, billed to you. There is still no AUI server in between, and AUI never sees your key.

It remembers privately. Chats and memory are saved in this browser, encrypted if you add a passphrase. No ads, no analytics profile. An account is optional.

Teach it how you work.

A skill is a short set of instructions that AUI follows in every answer. Turn one on for a chat, or keep it always on.

Twenty-six come built in. They summarize, proofread, translate, review code, quiz you, make flashcards, coach math, writing and languages, and take meeting notes. Homework Helper guides you to your own answer instead of handing one over. Write your own for the jobs you repeat, share one as a link, or import a SKILL.md file.

Built for studying.

Everything here is free, and it all stays in your browser.

Before a test

Ask AUI to quiz you, then turn any answer into flashcards. Cards come back after 1, 3 and 7 days, so they stick.

Writing an essay

Essay feedback comments on your thesis, evidence and structure. It points at what to fix and never rewrites your sentences. Make APA, MLA or Chicago citations without guessing the punctuation.

Stuck on a problem

The math coach asks what you tried, then gives one step at a time. Save a wrong answer to your mistake notebook and try it again later.

Planning exams

Enter each exam date and its topics. AUI builds a day-by-day plan you can add to your calendar, and keeps a streak that forgives one missed day.

Take it to your desktop.

The desktop app opens AUI in its own window, with the same chats and skills. Press Ctrl+Shift+Space from anywhere to open or hide it, drag over part of your screen to ask about it, and keep it in the tray. It updates itself.

Download for Windows, Mac or Linux

You can also install AUI straight from Chrome or Edge: look for the install icon in the address bar.
