Your AI, on your own key.

AUI connects to the model you choose through your own OpenRouter key. No AUI server sits between you and the model, and AUI itself is free.

Start chatting Continue with Google

No AUI account needed. Paste your OpenRouter key once; it stays in your browser. Prefer to stay offline? Switch to a small model on your device in Settings.

Your key. Your chats. Your call.

You bring the key. Messages go from your browser to OpenRouter and the model you pick, billed to you. There is no AUI server reading them, and AUI never sees your key.

It can stay offline. Choose the model on this device and nothing leaves your computer. It is smaller, but free and private.

It remembers privately. Chats and memory are saved in this browser, encrypted if you add a passphrase. No ads, no analytics profile. An account is optional.

Teach it how you work.

A skill is a short set of instructions that AUI follows in every answer. Turn one on for a chat, or keep it always on.

Thirteen come built in, including a Homework Helper that guides you without giving the answer: summarizing, proofreading, translating, reviewing code, tutoring, meeting notes and more. Write your own for the jobs you repeat, or import a SKILL.md file.

Three plans. None of them charges you.

AUI is free. Plans change how many custom skills you can keep and add a badge to your profile. The checkout is a demo: it checks that a card number is well formed with the Luhn test, takes no money, and keeps only the card brand and last four digits.

Take it to your desktop.

The desktop app opens AUI in its own window, with the same chats and skills. Add your OpenRouter key once in the app. It updates itself when the website does.
