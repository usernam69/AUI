Security

Report a problem

Found a security problem? Email supportaton@gmail.com with the steps to reproduce it. Please don't open a public issue for security problems. You'll get a reply within a week, and a fix as soon as one is ready.

In scope: the AUI website, the Beta pages and the account and sync features. Out of scope: denial-of-service, spam, and problems in browsers or third-party services.

Thank you

People who report a real problem responsibly are thanked here (with their permission).

How AUI protects you

  • Replies come from a small model on your device, or from OpenRouter if you add your own key (saved only in your browser). AUI's own servers never see your messages.
  • A Content Security Policy only lets scripts load from AUI and two pinned library hosts; model replies are sanitized before they're shown.
  • Code you run from a reply runs in a sandboxed frame with no access to AUI or your data.
  • Chats can be encrypted with a passphrase on your device. Accounts can use two-factor sign-in.
  • Database rows are protected by row-level security, so each account can only read its own data.
